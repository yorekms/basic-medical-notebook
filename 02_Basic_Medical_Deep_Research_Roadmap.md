# **Basic Medical research roadmap for the next 100 topics**

This roadmap turns the original list of 100 additional topics into a sequence of research questions. Fifty topics now have source checked deep references. The other 50 remain planned. Proposed species and method questions describe work still needed; they are not validation claims or clinical recommendations.

The twelve starter briefs remain available separately. Together, the starter set and the completed expansion batches provide documents for 62 of the 112 cataloged test families. This count describes this collection, not the proportion of all known medical tests.
